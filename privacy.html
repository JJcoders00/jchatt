Zero-Knowledge Privacy Policy

How JChatt achieves mathematical privacy: zero phone numbers, zero contact book scraping, zero metadata retention, and pure client-side key sovereignty.

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Version 2.4.0

Core Architectural Guarantee: Zero-Knowledge by Construction Under Section 6 & 8 of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and Article 25 of the EU GDPR (Data Protection by Design and by Default), JChatt is engineered so that we cannot access, read, decrypt, or sell your communications even under government subpoena, because private keys exist exclusively on your physical device.

1. Introduction & Statutory Context

This Privacy Policy governs your use of JChatt (accessible via web client at https://jjcodersjchatt.web.app and native Android binaries). JChatt is operated as a sovereign, zero-telemetry communications protocol. We adhere strictly to the highest global data privacy standards, including:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India): Comprehensive compliance with consent architectures, data minimization principles, and rights of Data Principals.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR): Articles 5, 6, 9, 25, and 32 governing cryptographic security and data minimization.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA): Zero sale or sharing of personal information.

3. Client-Side Cryptographic Identity

Your JChatt identity is generated locally in your browser or device memory using a 12-word cryptographic seed phrase derived from the BIP-39 standard dictionary.

  • No Central Directory: Your public handle (e.g. @name) is paired with a SHA-256 derived account token. JChatt servers hold only the cryptographic mapping required to route ciphertext between peers.
  • Key Sovereignty: Only you hold your 12-word mnemonic. JChatt employees, cloud hosts, and infrastructure providers do not possess your private keys and cannot recover lost accounts.

4. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) & Message Handling

Every message, image, voice recording, poll, and document sent through JChatt is sealed client-side before transmission:

  • Encryption Standard: WebCrypto AES-256-GCM with unique 12-byte cryptographic random IVs and PBKDF2/SHA-256 derived session keys.
  • Cloud Oblivion: On the Firebase Realtime Database cloud layer, the stored message payload contains strictly ciphertext formatted as enc:v2:aes:... and a generic placeholder "🔒 Encrypted Message". No plaintext content or raw image bytes ever touch the database.
  • Automatic Vanishing Timers: Messages configured with self-destruct timers (10 seconds, 1 minute, 1 hour, or 24 hours) are purged automatically from both local memory and remote storage upon timer expiration.

5. Peer-to-Peer Voice & Video Calling

JChatt voice and video calls utilize direct WebRTC mesh architecture:

  • Audio and video streams travel peer-to-peer (phone-to-phone) encrypted via DTLS-SRTP.
  • Signaling SDP exchanges are encrypted in temporary signaling queues that are wiped immediately upon call termination.
  • JChatt does not record, intercept, transcribe, or store audio/video data under any circumstances.

6. Camouflage & Anti-Coercion Features

To protect users living in environments with physical search threats:

  • Calculator Stealth Mode: Converts the app UI into a functioning arithmetic calculator. Only entering the user's secret code (e.g. 314159=) reveals the private vault.
  • Duress PIN Protocol: If forced to unlock under physical threat, entering a designated Duress PIN displays an authentic-looking Decoy Inbox. Real private chats remain cryptographically unmounted and undetectable.

7. Data Principal Rights under DPDPA 2023

Under Chapter III of the DPDPA 2023, you enjoy statutory rights regarding your digital persona:

  • Right to Erasure (Sec. 12): You can purge your entire conversation history, active identity token, and cloud mappings at any instant using "Clear Chat", "Delete Account", or "Self-Destruct Identity". Because data is not backed up in plaintext, deletion is irreversible and immediate.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Sec. 13): Should you have concerns regarding metadata routing, our Data Protection Officer is reachable at the contact provided below.
  • Right to Nominate (Sec. 14): You may pass your 12-word mnemonic to any designated nominee offline; JChatt requires no institutional transfer.
