Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JChatt's security, identity, and compliance.

How do I recover my account without a phone number?

When you register, JChatt gives you a 12-word cryptographic seed phrase generated on your device. Write these 12 words down safely. Entering this phrase on any phone or browser instantly restores your account.

Can JChatt or servers read my messages or listen to calls?

Mathematically impossible. All messages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and calls use WebRTC DTLS-SRTP before leaving your hardware. Servers only ever transmit ciphertext envelopes.

How does the Anti-Coercion Calculator Disguise work?

When enabled in Security Settings, JChatt masquerades as a functioning scientific calculator. Typing standard math performs calculations. Entering your secret code (e.g. 314159=) instantly unlocks your secure vault.

Is JChatt completely free?

Yes. 100% free of charge. No subscriptions, no ads, and no paywalled features. We believe sovereign privacy is a universal human right.
