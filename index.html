Questions

Questions people ask first

Do I need a phone number or an email?

No. JChatt never asks for a phone number, email or SIM. Your identity is made on your device from a 12-word recovery phrase, or you can start with an instant burner identity.

What happens if I lose my 12 words?

Your phrase is the only key to your identity, and it never leaves your device. Without it, nobody can restore your account, including us. Write it on paper and keep it somewhere safe.

How does the duress PIN work?

You set a second PIN. If someone forces you to unlock JChatt, entering it opens a realistic decoy inbox instead of your real chats. The real chats stay hidden and untouched.

Are calls really phone-to-phone?

Calls use WebRTC to connect your devices directly and are encrypted with DTLS-SRTP, with acoustic echo cancellation for clear audio.

Can I use JChatt on my computer?

Yes. Open JChatt Web in a desktop browser. It works alongside the Android app with the same identity.
